My Story

01

The Curious Creative

From an early age, I was drawn to communication, leadership, and public speaking. Whether addressing audiences, participating in discussions, or contributing through society activities, I discovered the power of ideas and the impact they can create when shared effectively. These experiences helped me develop confidence, teamwork, and the ability to connect with people from diverse backgrounds. Over time, this passion for communication evolved into a desire to solve real-world problems through technology and innovation.

02

Exploring Technology Through Projects

My fascination with technology led me to explore software development and engineering beyond the classroom. I started building projects that challenged me to learn new technologies and think like a problem solver. From developing an Amazon Clone with full-stack functionality to creating a real-time Flight Tracking platform, every project strengthened my understanding of modern development practices. Along the way, I discovered my passion for designing intuitive user experiences, combining functionality with clean and engaging interfaces.

03

Research, Development & Impact

Today, I am expanding my technical expertise through my internship at DRDO, where I am working on advanced engineering and safety-oriented solutions. One of my key projects is a Hazard Zone Detection System designed to analyze and visualize potential risk areas through simulation and intelligent data processing. Alongside this, I continue refining my previous projects while exploring new ideas at the intersection of software engineering, design, and research. My goal is to build technology that is not only innovative but also meaningful, practical, and impactful.
